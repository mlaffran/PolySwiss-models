PolySwiss — privacy

Test version (TestFlight). Last updated 23 October 2026 (app parameters about every hour, also in the background; voice messages; forums and classrooms).

Who

PolySwiss is developed by Marco Laffranchi, Switzerland. Contact: the feedback function in the app (Main → Feedback to PolySwiss, with a pseudonym so that we can answer).

What stays on your phone

  • Your identities (names, languages, age band), your settings, the words learned from your own writing (only if you switch learning on), your Address list and chats kept on the phone. They are stored in encrypted files (AES-256-GCM); the key is in the phone's keychain. Profile photos (yours and those your contacts share with you) and attachments you open are kept as ordinary files on the phone.
  • Word suggestions, the translator and dictation run on the phone. What you type or say is not sent anywhere for them.
  • Language packages (word-suggestion models, dictionaries, dictation models) are downloaded from GitHub and stored on the phone. Downloading them sends nothing about you beyond what any download does (your IP address reaches GitHub).
  • Check age (Main) asks your phone's system — Apple's Declared Age Range on iOS 26, Google Play's Age Signals on Android — for your age range, only if you agree in the system's own window. Only the band (under 13, 13+, 16+, 18+) is kept, on the phone; nothing is sent to PolySwiss. Without it you set the band yourself (at most twice in 30 days).

What leaves your phone

  • Messages to other people go through the PolySwiss server (matrix.polyswiss.app, in Switzerland) with the Matrix account of your identity — a random, pseudonymous address. Texts, files, the set-up of calls, profile photos, the names you give each other and parent-child roles are end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key that only your two phones can compute, made when you connected with the QR code. The server forwards them but cannot read them; it does see which accounts write to each other and when. PolySwiss does not send a message to someone you are not connected with. Calls are encrypted with the WebRTC standard (DTLS-SRTP).
  • Forums and classrooms (identity 5 and identity 4): a private one is joined only with its QR invitation; its name, photo, the members' names and its messages are end-to-end encrypted with a key that only its members have, which the server never sees. A public one is listed for everybody and anyone can join, so its name, description and messages can also be read by the server, like a notice board.
  • No IP addresses kept. The PolySwiss server does not keep IP addresses: not in its database, its logs or its backups. During a call the relay server needs the addresses to connect the two phones, but writes them nowhere.
  • Budget given to others (Address, Parental link, Shop → Give budget), with its note if you write one, is signed and encrypted for the receiver; the server sees that a gift was sent, not the amount or the note.
  • Parent-child link (optional, Main → Parental link / Child link): parent and child connect with the QR code using a separate account for identity G. Both see the same entries — links, money given, the child's budget low or empty — never messages or calls. When the child becomes 18+, the links end and that account is deleted on the server.
  • Feedback you send to PolySwiss: anonymous, or with a pseudonym (a random key of this phone) so that we can answer. It is encrypted so that only PolySwiss can read it. Everything sent is listed in the app under "Data sent to PolySwiss".
  • About every hour, anonymous app parameters (every 60 minutes ± 20 %, also when the app is closed): the credit in the PolySwiss wallet, the price per week of your own subscriptions and of your linked children's, your age band, how many identity subscriptions and which other kinds are active, the installed language packages, how many parents and children are linked, how many minutes the app was open since the previous sending, and a control number that lets PolySwiss check the values were not altered. No names, accounts, texts, operating system or device model. Also listed under "Data sent to PolySwiss".

PolySwiss has no advertising, no tracking and no analytics service, and sells no data.

Deleting your data

"Quit PolySwiss and delete all" (Main → Subscriptions) signs every identity out and deletes your data from the phone. Deleting the app deletes it too. To have feedback you sent deleted, ask through the feedback function (with a pseudonym).
